## Known traceability gaps

- The TIGER/Line layers (`cd119`, `sldu`, `sldl`, `county`, `place`, `unsd`) were loaded before this project tracked sources: no URL or vintage is stored. Checked 2026-10-28: Maryland's state legislative districts in the database match TIGER 2026 (`tl_2026_24_sldl`) to within 0.19 km² per district, i.e. the 2022 plan in force. The other TIGER layers have not been compared yet.
- Hand-transcribed loaders (Montgomery primary ballot style 125) point at the certified PDF, not at a page number.
